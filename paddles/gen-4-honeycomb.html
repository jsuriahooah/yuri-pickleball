Gen 4 Honeycomb pickleball paddlesThe progression paddles.

Great comfort and control to grow your game.

Every YURI paddle starts with a high-end carbon face and great design components.

Gen 4 Honeycomb is a honeycomb core with filled cells. These honeycomb pickleball paddles pair it with a smooth Toray T800 carbon face.

Why we call it Gen 4.

Plain honeycomb has open cells. Our Gen 4 Honeycomb fills those cells.
