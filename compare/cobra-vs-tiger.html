Cobra vs Tiger

Same core, face and shape. You pick the thickness: 16 mm or 14 mm.

What changes

Cobra leans to power. Tiger leans to control. Both wear the Signature Black & White look.

Who each one suits

Hybrid +Power

Cobra

For relaxed game play. It adds pace to an easy swing, at every level.

Hybrid +Control

Tiger

For aggressive game play. A control paddle helps you aim your big swings, at every level.
