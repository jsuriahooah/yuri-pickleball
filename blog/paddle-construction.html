Paddle construction

How a YURI paddle is built.

Every YURI paddle starts with a Toray T800 carbon face. Here's what sits under it, core by core.

We make five cores across four lines. Each one changes how the ball comes off the face. If you like to know what's inside before you play it, this page is for you.

Layer by layer.

Every YURI paddle has the same simple build: a carbon face on each side of the core, with the sides sealed in resin so no layer is left open. The edge guard goes on over that seal. The core in the middle is what changes from line to line.

Face, surface, thickness.

Three choices sit on top of the core. Here's what each one does.

The face: Toray T800 carbon.

Every face on every YURI paddle is Toray T800 carbon. Toray rates T800 stiffer than the T700 grade most paddles use. It's a real upgrade from your starter paddle.

Carbon fiber paddles, explained

Smooth or 120 Grit.

Five paddles come smooth or with a 120 Grit surface for extra spin. The six Signature paddles have the 120 Grit surface too. The name is a nod to 120 grit sandpaper: a fine texture applied to the face grabs the ball for extra spin. Only the face changes: the core, thickness and shape stay the same.

Smooth or 120 Grit: read the guide

16 mm or 14 mm.

The 16 mm models give more power. The 14 mm models give more control. Every Signature core comes in both.

16 mm or 14 mm: read the guide

Five cores, five feels.

Same carbon face. Pick the core that fits how you play.

Vortex

How it plays: Power. The 16 mm Vortex Pro is our power paddle; the 14 mm adds a little control.

Cobra, Tiger

How it plays: Control. It helps you aim your big swings.

Foam Tech

Foam Tech

Our Gen 4 foam core. A foam core fills the paddle with foam instead of honeycomb cells.

How it plays: Pop and control.

Bazooka 120 Grit, Purple Reign 120 Grit, Blaze Blue 120 Grit, Blaze Blue, Bazooka, Purple Reign, Golden Pickle

Gen 4 Honeycomb

Gen 4 Honeycomb

A honeycomb core with filled cells. Plain honeycomb has open cells; our Gen 4 Honeycomb fills them. In the Signature line it's called Charged Hive.

How it plays: Great comfort and control.

Jett Black 120 Grit, Cherry Bomb 120 Grit, Pink & Chic, Pink & Chic - Phat, Jett Black, Cherry Bomb

Charged Hive is our Signature name for the Gen 4 Honeycomb core: the same honeycomb with every cell filled.

Shape, size and weight.

Most YURI paddles are elongated: 16.4 × 7.44 in on the Signature line, 16.5 × 7.5 in on Foam Tech and Gen 4 Honeycomb. Golden Pickle and Pink & Chic - Phat are widebody paddles at 16 × 8 in, and our lightest at 7.6 oz. Handles are 5.5 in, or 5.25 in on the two widebodies.
